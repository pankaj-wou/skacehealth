# Specialities page — 4 October 2026

Source: client-supplied "Our Specialities" copy. Stored in `src/data/specialities.ts` and rendered by `components/site/specialities.tsx` on its own server route (`app/specialities/page.tsx`).

Editorial changes were limited to sentence-case list items, "superspeciality" (site spelling), "Neuro-critical care" / "Neuro-rehabilitation", and expanding unit acronyms once. No services or claims were added.

Each programme links to its existing `/specialities/<slug>` page and to the booking form with that speciality preselected. Specialities not covered by the new copy (nephrology, general medicine, anaesthesiology, gastroenterology, plastic surgery, oral & maxillofacial surgery, ENT) stay listed under "Other specialities" because doctor profiles reference them.

The new copy lists Urology separately from General & Minimal Access Surgery; the site-wide six service departments (Home, footer, About) still group urology under General Surgery.
